- **Song change peek:** when a new song starts, the closed notch briefly shows its cover and title. Choose how long it stays in Settings → Music.
- **Volume & brightness in the notch:** changes show beside the notch. With Accessibility allowed, Lenotch can replace the macOS pop-up entirely.
- **Mic & camera indicator:** an orange outline while an app uses the microphone, green for the camera, both during a video call. An optional glow is available too.
- **Timer:** start one from the notch (turn it on in Settings → Timer). It counts down beside the notch, next to your music, and folds down with an alarm when it ends. Silent timers are also possible.
- **Network speed:** download and upload speed shows beside the notch while a big transfer runs.
- **Crypto ticker:** Bitcoin, Ethereum and more beside the closed notch when nothing is playing (off by default, Settings → Crypto).
- **What's New:** after an update, Lenotch shows what changed, and the update dialog now includes release notes.
- **Tidier Settings:** pages are sorted into Notch, Features, Beside the Notch and Privacy.
